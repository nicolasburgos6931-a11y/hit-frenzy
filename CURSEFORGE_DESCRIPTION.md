# Hit Frenzy

**Turn every fight into an arcade combo.**

Hit a mob and the exact damage you dealt floats up above it. Keep hitting and your combo counter (x2, x3, x4...) grows bigger with every strike and flashes through frantic rainbow colors. Stop fighting and the combo ends with a verdict: a sad "Meh..." for a tiny combo, or a huge "AMAZING!!" if you really went wild.

## Features
- Floating damage numbers on every hit
- Special popups for critical hits ("CRITICAL!"), weak hits ("Meh...") and kills ("KILL!!")
- Combo counter that grows and flashes through rainbow colors as it climbs
- Mid-combo messages at x5, x10, x20, x30 and x50
- End-of-combo rating based on how many hits you landed: Meh... / Nice! / GREAT! / AMAZING!!
- Sounds and particles that get more intense as your combo grows
- English and Spanish built in, with random messages for variety
- Fully configurable: texts, colors, timings, thresholds, sounds, particles (config/hitfrenzy.properties)

## Requirements
- Minecraft 26.3 (Fabric)
- Fabric API
- Java 25

## Multiplayer
Install it on the server. Players do not need to install anything.
